Campaign overview01—04

Terrain changed the rules.

The conflict unfolded along high ridgelines in the Kargil–Dras–Batalik region. Positions overlooking the Srinagar–Leh road created a direct threat to a critical line of communication. Indian forces mounted a sustained campaign to retake occupied heights while keeping the conflict on India's side of the Line of Control.

!

Casualty figures vary by source and scope.

This dossier retains the long-cited figure of 527 Indian fatalities in the Kargil War. The National War Memorial's Operation Vijay page reports 674 Indian soldiers lost during the operation. Those figures should not be silently treated as identical: check each source's scope and wording before quoting a number.

↗

Sortie counts are category-dependent.

Government summaries use categories such as strike, reconnaissance/electronic intelligence, escort, helicopter, air transport, and casualty evacuation. These category totals are not necessarily comparable with an all-missions sortie count from a different account.

How to read this archive

Read critically

Source cards identify the publishing institution and explain what each reference is best used for. The dossier combines structured historical summaries with selected official material. Interpretive claims, disputed details, and differing totals should be checked against the linked original sources. This is an educational research tool, not an official government publication.
